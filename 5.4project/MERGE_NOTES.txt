Combined behavior
  Retains B's five pitch/harmony modes, KEY2 controls, PCM producer queue,
  boosted saturated volume, compact audio history and DLT sector guard.
  Enables B early photo prefetch while its protected sender slot is read.
  Enables A hidden thumbnail preparation during transitions, including
  source-slot protection, cancellation acknowledgement and frame publication.
  Retains original 48-step transitions, A three photo slots and B two slots.
  Includes the A/B millisecond timing display from the thumbnail branch.
  Once a successful photo timing exists, timing pages take normal display
  priority; B KEY2 pitch controls remain active. Error diagnostics retain
  priority. See SWITCH_TIMING.txt for measurement boundaries.

Conflict resolution
  B photo_sd_cache instance retains 5.4 audio_stream_reset and enables
  EARLY_PREFETCH. B TD sources include both pitch modules AND timing RTL.
  No wiring, image pixel format or board-link protocol changes are required.
